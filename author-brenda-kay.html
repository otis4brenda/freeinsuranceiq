Why FreeInsuranceIQ Exists

Almost every insurance resource online is designed to collect your contact information. Quote forms, comparison sites, lead generators — they're built to route you to a salesperson, not to answer your question.

Brenda built FreeInsuranceIQ to give people the number first — so you can walk into any insurance conversation knowing roughly what you should be paying, how much coverage you actually need, and whether what you're being quoted is reasonable. No email required. No follow-up call. Just the math.

She writes and reviews all editorial content on FreeInsuranceIQ, drawing on her own experience navigating insurance decisions as a property manager, landlord, and small business owner.
