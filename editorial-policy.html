Editorial Policy & Methodology

How we research, build, and maintain our insurance calculators.

Our Editorial Mission

FreeInsuranceIQ is committed to providing accurate, unbiased, and up-to-date insurance education tools. Insurance is a YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) topic — decisions made based on poor information can have serious financial consequences. That's why our editorial team holds all content to a strict standard of factual accuracy, source transparency, and independence from commercial interests.

We do not accept payment from insurance carriers, brokers, or any third party to influence our calculator outputs or editorial content. Our only revenue is Google AdSense display advertising, which has no bearing on the tools we build or the information we present.

How Our Calculators Are Built

Every calculator on FreeInsuranceIQ is developed through a research-first process:

  • Industry data sourcing: Premium benchmarks, coverage cost ranges, and actuarial rules of thumb are sourced from NAIC (National Association of Insurance Commissioners) reports, LIMRA industry studies, Genworth Cost of Care surveys, and J.D. Power consumer research.
  • Government data integration: Federal Poverty Level guidelines (for ACA subsidy calculations), USCIS statistics, SSA disability data, and state-level regulation data are sourced directly from the relevant government agencies.
  • Actuarial validation: Coverage need calculations follow established actuarial guidelines (DIME method for life insurance, replacement cost valuation for home insurance) published by industry bodies including the American Academy of Actuaries.
  • Consumer protection standards: All calculator guidance aligns with NAIC consumer education standards and state insurance department recommendations.

Primary Data Sources

Our insurance calculators draw on the following authoritative sources:

  • NAIC (National Association of Insurance Commissioners) — Premium data, market conduct reports, and consumer protection guidelines used across all calculator benchmarks.
  • LIMRA — Life insurance ownership statistics, industry benchmarks, and coverage gap research referenced in our Life Insurance Calculator.
  • Genworth Cost of Care Survey — Long-term care cost benchmarks by state used in our Long-Term Care Cost Calculator.
  • Healthcare.gov / CMS — ACA subsidy eligibility rules, Federal Poverty Level thresholds, and plan premium benchmarks used in our Health Insurance Subsidy Calculator.
  • Social Security Administration (SSA) — SSDI approval rates, benefit amounts, and disability statistics referenced in our Disability Insurance Calculator.
  • J.D. Power Insurance Studies — Consumer satisfaction data and carrier rating benchmarks referenced in editorial guidance.
  • Insurance Information Institute (III) — Home and auto insurance premium benchmarks, claims frequency data, and consumer education resources.

Content Update Schedule

Our editorial team maintains the following review schedule:

Annual Updates (minimum)

Every January, we review all calculators against newly published Federal Poverty Level guidelines, updated ACA marketplace premium data, and NAIC annual market reports.

Quarterly Updates

Premium benchmarks, coverage cost ranges, and state fee data are reviewed quarterly to reflect current market conditions. Insurance premiums are sensitive to interest rates, claims environments, and regulatory changes — we update when material shifts occur.

Event-Driven Updates

When ACA open enrollment rules change, when USCIS updates fee schedules, or when major actuarial benchmarks shift, we update affected calculators promptly. All pages display a "Last updated" date to indicate when content was last reviewed.

Accuracy & Corrections Policy

Insurance cost estimates are inherently approximate — your actual premium depends on dozens of factors specific to you, your location, your health history, and the insurer you choose. Our calculators are designed to give you a realistic ballpark, not a guaranteed quote.

If you believe a calculator contains an error, please contact us at support@freeinsuranceiq.com with the subject "Calculator Error." We investigate all reported errors and publish corrections promptly.

Important: Before purchasing any insurance policy, always get quotes from multiple licensed insurers and consult a licensed insurance professional in your state. For health insurance, visit healthcare.gov for official enrollment and subsidy eligibility information.
